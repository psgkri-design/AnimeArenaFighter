  horns, demon wings/tail, mage hat/staff, templar cape/armor/weapon,
  bunny ears/techwear/orange accents.
- Rebuilt main menu as a landscape BATTLE SETUP screen.
- Added independent YOUR FIGHTER and OPPONENT selection.
- Added fighter role, description, stats and skill-list panels.
- Added Easy / Normal / Hard AI selector.
- Added Character Select return path from pause and results screens.
- Increased arena decorative pillar count and refined lighting/background values.
- Updated match startup to preserve selected player, enemy and AI difficulty.
- Next target: integrate higher-quality licensed external 3D character assets and animation sets.
